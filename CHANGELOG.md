# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha.1

First alpha of Muzik for Android. Muzik now runs on the phone, so YouTube sees your own IP address. It replaces the old self-hosted web app and needs no server.

- Search YouTube Music for songs, albums, and playlists.
- Stream with background playback, lock-screen controls, and Bluetooth controls.
- Like songs and build playlists on the phone.
- Download songs, albums, and playlists for offline play.
- Follow an album or playlist. Muzik checks it every 12 hours and downloads new tracks.
- Read synced lyrics from LRCLIB. Tap a line to jump to it.
- Use a navigation rail and side-by-side panes on tablets and foldables.

Known limits:

- Downloads stay private to Muzik and have no tags.
- Android does not keep the queue after it stops the app.
- If YouTube blocks your network, try another network, such as mobile data.

Requires Android 8.0 or later. Install `muzik-0.1.0-alpha.1.apk` and check it against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
